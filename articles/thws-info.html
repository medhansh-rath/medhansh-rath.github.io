1. Opening a Bank Account

Upon arriving in Germany, setting up a Girokonto (checking account) is essential for paying rent and managing expenses.

Sparkasse Support via THWS

THWS has an official tie-up with Sparkasse Schweinfurt, making account setup hassle-free for students. To request assistance, send an email to:

welcome@thws.de
Note: THWS cannot assist with opening accounts at Sparkasse in Würzburg.
Online Banking Alternative

Digital banks like N26 allow quick setup (~10 minutes) without needing an immediate Anmeldung (city registration).

Cash Withdrawals & CashGroup

Always use your bank's own ATMs to avoid fees. If you use a CashGroup bank (Commerzbank, Deutsche Bank, HypoVereinsbank, Postbank), you can withdraw fee-free from any CashGroup ATM.

2. Dorms, Housing & Rental Contracts

Critical Warning: Most leases require a 3-month notice period for cancellation. If you move out early without proper notice, rent will be deducted directly from your security deposit!

Understanding German Rent Terms

Kaltmiete: Base rent excluding utilities (water, electricity, heating).
Warmmiete: Total rent including utility advance payments.

3. City Registration & GEZ Fee

City Registration (Anmeldung) Process

Upon moving to Schweinfurt, you must register your residential address at the City Hall (Rathaus at Marktplatz 1). Follow this specific two-step workflow:

  1. First, visit the Foreigners' Office (Ausländerbehörde) at Brückenstraße 14 to get an official stamp on your address registration application form.
  2. Next, go to the Rathaus. No prior appointment is required—take an appointment number ticket from the machine inside. Average wait time is only 5–10 minutes.

*If in temporary housing without a Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (landlord confirmation), consult Rathaus staff directly.

GEZ (Rundfunkbeitrag)

Germany charges a mandatory broadcast fee of €18.36 per month per household for public radio/TV.

  • Cost is split evenly among all flatmates sharing an apartment.
  • Only one person per household registers and handles payments (quarterly, semi-annually, or annually).

4. Campus Navigation & Printers

THWS room codes use a three-number/letter convention (e.g., 1.E.08):

1st Digit = Building 1–8: Campus 1
9: Campus 2
20–21: Campus 3
2nd Code = Floor E = Erdgeschoss (Ground Floor)
1, 2... = Upper Floors
U = Basement
3rd Digit = Room Specific room number on that floor.

6. Exams & Academic Regulations

  • Attempt Rules: You have a maximum of 3 attempts per exam.
    • Failing Attempt 1 requires retaking in the next semester.
    • Failing Attempt 2 grants a 1-year window for the 3rd attempt.
    • Failing Attempt 3 results in immediate exmatriculation.
  • Non-Attendance: If you miss an exam without formal deregistration or a valid medical certificate, it counts as an automatic fail grade (5.0).
  • Attempt Deadlines:
    • Semesters 1 & 2 subjects must be taken by 3rd Subject Semester (*2 subjects must be taken by 2nd semester).
    • Semesters 3 & 4 subjects by 6th Subject Semester.
    • Semesters 5–7 subjects by 9th Subject Semester.
  • Grade Improvement: 3–4 attempts are granted for grade improvement. If you do not improve, the attempt is discarded.

7. Healthcare & General Practitioners

In Germany, your primary healthcare contact is a Hausarzt (General Practitioner). They operate Mon–Fri. On weekends or evenings, visit a hospital emergency room.
